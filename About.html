About Access 2 Justice

Law affects everyone.
Understanding it should not be reserved for a few.

Access 2 Justice is an undergraduate-led organization built on the belief that legal knowledge should be accessible long before someone enters a courtroom, hires a lawyer, or applies to law school.

Not a pre-law club. A community built around access, education and impact.

Why It Matters

The law is already part of your life.

You do not have to want to become a lawyer for legal literacy to matter. The law shapes the jobs we work, the homes we rent, the businesses we build, the contracts we sign, the education we receive and the rights we rely on.

Yet many people only begin learning how legal systems work when they are already facing a problem.

Access 2 Justice exists because understanding your rights, responsibilities and available resources should not depend on your career path, background or access to professional networks.

Our Story

A2J began with a question: why does legal knowledge feel so inaccessible?

Access 2 Justice was created at the University of Calgary from a desire to make conversations about law and justice more understandable, approachable and useful to ordinary people.

Where We Started

Too often, legal education begins only after someone decides they want to become a lawyer, or after a person is already dealing with a legal issue.

We saw an opportunity to create something different: an organization where students from every discipline could learn about the systems that affect their lives while also contributing to meaningful access-to-justice work.

A2J grew from conversations between students into a broader initiative focused on education, service, professional connection and community engagement.

What We Are Building

We are building a space where undergraduate students can understand the law without needing a legal background, develop meaningful professional skills and participate in projects that extend beyond the classroom.

That means workshops that make legal topics easier to understand, volunteer projects that connect students to the community, events that bring people into conversations about justice and partnerships that create real opportunities.

Our goal is not simply to talk about access to justice. It is to actively contribute to it.

Who We Are For

You do not need to be pre-law to belong here.

A2J is intentionally interdisciplinary. Justice intersects with nearly every field, and our community is stronger when students bring different perspectives, experiences and areas of study into the conversation.

Business Students

Understand contracts, employment, corporate responsibility, consumer protection and the legal environment surrounding business.

Health & Science Students

Explore questions involving consent, privacy, ethics, regulation, professional responsibility and public policy.

Arts & Social Sciences

Connect law to politics, sociology, inequality, public policy, human rights and community institutions.

Students From Every Faculty

Build practical knowledge about rights, systems and resources that matter regardless of your degree or future profession.
