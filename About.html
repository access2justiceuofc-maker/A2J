About Access 2 Justice

Law affects everyone.
Understanding it should too.

Access 2 Justice is a student-led organization focused on making legal knowledge more accessible, creating meaningful opportunities for students, and building stronger connections between campus, community, and the legal sector.

Our Story

Why A2J was created.

Access 2 Justice began with a simple idea: legal knowledge should not only become important when someone enters law school or finds themselves facing a legal problem.

The law shapes everyday life — our workplaces, housing, education, businesses, rights, responsibilities and communities.

A2J was created to make those conversations easier to access while giving undergraduate students meaningful ways to learn, volunteer, build skills and contribute to the community.

Our Mission

Make justice more understandable and accessible.

Our mission is to strengthen legal literacy by creating accessible educational opportunities, community initiatives and meaningful student experiences.

We bring students together with professionals, organizations and community partners to learn about the systems that shape everyday life and help make those systems easier to understand.

A2J is not only for students pursuing law. It is for anyone who believes people should better understand the rights, responsibilities and systems that affect them.
