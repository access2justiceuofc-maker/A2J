About Access 2 Justice

Built by students.
Designed to make justice more accessible.

Access 2 Justice is an undergraduate-led organization dedicated to making legal knowledge, professional opportunities and access-to-justice conversations more understandable, accessible and meaningful for students and the wider community.

Why We Exist

Legal knowledge should not belong only to people already inside the profession.

A2J was created around a simple belief: students should not have to wait until law school or a legal career to understand the institutions, rights and systems that shape their lives.

Our Story

Access 2 Justice began with conversations among University of Calgary undergraduate students about the gap between the legal system and the people expected to navigate it.

Legal information can often feel technical, intimidating or inaccessible. At the same time, many undergraduate students who are interested in law, policy, justice or public service struggle to find meaningful ways to participate before entering professional school.

A2J was created to help close both gaps.

What began as a student initiative has grown into a platform for legal literacy, volunteer service, professional development, mentorship, community partnerships and practical opportunities for students to contribute to access-to-justice work.

What We Believe

Access to justice is about more than access to a courtroom or a lawyer. It also means understanding your rights, knowing where to seek help, being able to understand legal information and feeling capable of participating in systems that affect your life.

We believe undergraduate students can play a meaningful role in this work through education, outreach, service, research and collaboration.

A2J creates opportunities for students to learn from legal professionals while also contributing to projects that have a tangible impact beyond the classroom.

Our Mission

Build legal literacy. Create opportunity. Strengthen access to justice.

Our mission is to bridge the space between undergraduate education, the legal profession and the broader community through accessible education, meaningful service and practical opportunities.

Membership

What do you actually get when you join A2J?

Membership is designed to give students more than another club name on a résumé. We want members to leave A2J with experience, connections, skills and meaningful contributions they can point to.

01

Volunteer Opportunities

Participate in A2J events, legal-literacy initiatives, community outreach, fundraisers, public education projects and partner organization opportunities.

02

Internship Opportunities

Members may receive access to internship, placement and experiential opportunities shared through A2J's professional and community network.

03

Signed Certificates

Eligible members can receive signed certificates recognizing meaningful volunteer involvement, project participation and contributions to A2J initiatives.

04

Professional Networking

Meet lawyers, professors, legal organizations, community leaders and other professionals through panels, workshops, events, collaborations and mentorship opportunities.

05

Hands-On Experience

Help plan real initiatives, contribute to public-facing projects, support community outreach and build transferable skills in research, communication, leadership and event development.

06

Member-Only Opportunities

Receive early access to selected events, volunteer positions, workshops, professional opportunities and initiatives made available through A2J and its partners.

More Than Membership

Build experience that means something.

Contribute to real projects

Members can support initiatives in legal literacy, outreach, community engagement, education, fundraising and access to justice.

Develop professional skills

Strengthen communication, teamwork, research, leadership, event planning, advocacy and professional relationship-building skills.

Discover pathways into law and justice

Learn about legal careers, law school, public-interest work, community legal organizations and alternative justice-related careers.

Create measurable impact

A2J is designed around tangible initiatives. Members are encouraged to contribute to projects they can meaningfully describe in future applications, interviews and professional opportunities.
