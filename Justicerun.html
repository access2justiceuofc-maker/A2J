Choose the option that matches how you want to participate. You can register individually, register a team, or join a team after receiving the team information from your captain.

Best for participants registering and fundraising on their own.

  1. Click Register and choose your ticket category.
  2. Complete your participant information and required acknowledgements.
  3. Create or join your fundraising campaign when prompted.
  4. Watch your email for race-package, waiver and event-day instructions.

More than a race

A movement for access to justice.

The Justice Run brings Calgary together around one simple idea: understanding your rights and accessing legal resources should not be out of reach.

Students, families, professionals, runners, walkers and community members are invited to move together while raising awareness and funds for initiatives connected to access to justice and legal literacy.

In recognition of Persons Day

Honouring those who challenged the limits of the law.

The Justice Run takes place during the week of Persons Day, recognizing the historic Persons Case and the Famous Five, whose advocacy helped advance women's legal recognition in Canada.

Their work reminds us that legal change begins when people challenge barriers, understand their rights and push institutions toward greater equality and justice.

Finish line experience

The run does not end at the finish line.

The finish area is expected to include participant services and small activations before guests continue to the main Justice Run Community Celebration.

  • Medal distribution
  • Hydration and recovery elements
  • Photo opportunities
  • Smaller activities and partner activations
  • Clear transition to Central Commons Park in University District
Important: The route is preliminary and remains subject to final City of Calgary permitting, safety review and operational approval. A complete Participant Guide with final race-day information will be released closer to the event.
