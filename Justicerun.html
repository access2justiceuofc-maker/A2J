More Than A Race

Justice starts with access.

The Justice Run is a community fundraising event created by Undergraduate Access to Justice to bring people together around legal awareness, education and meaningful community impact.


Whether you are a student, runner, walker, professional, family member or community supporter, there is a place for you. No running experience is required.

One community. One movement.

Participants will come together for the 5K Justice Run or the 1K Community Walk before joining us for a community celebration featuring speakers, activities, prizes, partners and opportunities to learn more about access to justice.

Why It Matters

Understanding your rights should not be a privilege.

The legal system affects employment, housing, family life, education, personal safety, business and many of the decisions people make every day. Yet many people do not know where to begin when they face a legal issue.

Legal Literacy

Helping people better understand their rights, responsibilities and the legal resources available to them.

Community Access

Creating stronger connections between students, professionals, organizations and the communities they serve.

Future Impact

Supporting initiatives and opportunities that can create lasting improvements in access to justice.

Honouring Persons Day

Moving forward because others challenged what was possible.

The Justice Run takes place during the week of Persons Day, commemorating the historic Persons Case and the Famous Five, whose advocacy helped advance women's legal recognition in Canada.


We recognize that the pursuit of justice continues. The Justice Run brings that history into the present by encouraging our community to learn, participate and help move access to justice forward.
