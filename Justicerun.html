Discover The Justice Run

More Than A Race

A movement for access to justice.

The Justice Run brings Calgary together around one simple idea: understanding your rights and accessing legal resources should not be out of reach.


Students, families, professionals, runners, walkers and community members are invited to move together while raising awareness and funds for initiatives connected to access to justice and legal literacy.

Run. Walk. Fundraise. Celebrate.

Participate in the 5K Justice Run or the 1K Community Walk, fundraise individually or as a team, and then join us at University District for a community celebration featuring speakers, prizes, partner activations and more.

Why We Run

Legal knowledge can change someone's life.

The law touches almost every part of our lives — employment, housing, education, family, personal safety, business and more. Yet navigating the legal system can be confusing and inaccessible.

Legal Literacy

Increasing awareness of legal rights, responsibilities and available resources.

Stronger Communities

Creating connections between students, professionals, organizations and the public.

Meaningful Impact

Raising funds and awareness for initiatives that help move access to justice forward.

In Recognition of Persons Day

Honouring those who challenged the limits of the law.

The Justice Run takes place during the week of Persons Day, commemorating the historic Persons Case and the Famous Five, whose advocacy helped advance women's legal recognition in Canada.


Their work reminds us that legal change begins when people challenge barriers, understand their rights and push institutions toward greater equality and justice.
